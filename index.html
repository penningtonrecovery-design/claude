Questions

Straight answers before you call.

I'm not techy — will I actually be able to run this?

Yes. It runs quietly in the background answering calls and booking jobs — you and your team don't need to learn new software. We handle setup and walk your office through anything they'll actually touch.

How long does it take to get up and running?

Most trade businesses are live within two weeks of the audit, connected to the phone lines and scheduling tools you already use.

Will this replace my dispatcher?

No — it takes the overflow and after-hours calls off their plate so they can focus on the calls that need a real person. Most owners tell us their dispatcher becomes far more effective, not replaced.

What happens to my customers' data?

It stays yours. Customer information is never used to train public AI models, and it's handled with the same care you'd expect from any business system you rely on daily.

I already use ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — does this replace it?

No — it plugs into what you already run. The goal is to catch what's slipping through the cracks around your existing tools, not rip and replace them.

What does this cost?

It depends on your call volume and what you need automated. Every engagement starts with the free audit, so you'll know the scope and cost before committing to anything.
